**Product:** The Immortalized Collection: 11 painted designs framed like vintage posters on the back.
Tees $27 · Hoodies $37 · Women's tees $27 · Desk mats $27.
**Cast:** Creed, Jax, Nova and Rio from the Elsewhere commercial cast sheet (AI characters).
**Formats:** 15 s vertical master (TikTok, Reels, Shorts, ads) + 6 s ad cutdown + 4 solo 7 s posts cut from the same footage.

## The idea

**"Some legends refuse to die."** Each painting comes to life, then we see who's wearing it.
The clothes are the payoff: every shot ends on a back print, held still long enough to read.

## How we keep the prints correct

1. **Every shot that shows a print is built from the real product photo.** The store's own
   "back print" mockup goes in as a reference, never a text description.
2. **Stills first, video second.** Stills are cheap (130 credits). I check every letter of
   MASTERLESS / LONG LIVE THE KING / QUEEN FOREVER / RISE FROM RUIN and the painting before any video is made.
3. **The turn uses two approved stills as the start and end frames.** The AI only invents the
   motion between two pictures we've already checked, not the print itself.
4. **Small motion.** No walking, no arm crossing. People stand; the camera, wind, steam and light do the moving.
5. **Every video gets the frame-by-frame check** we did on the Christmas clips. Any added text or
   warped print gets masked, or that moment is replaced by the real product close-up.
6. **The close-up (shot 3) and the end card are 100% real files**, so the commercial always shows
   the exact print people receive.
