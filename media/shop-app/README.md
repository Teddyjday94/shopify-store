# Shop app / brand settings kit (2026-11-12)

Brand settings can't be written through the Admin API; the store owner uploads these in
Settings > Brand (used by the Shop app, Shop Pay and Shopify Email) and in Sales channels > Shop.

- elsewhere-logo-light.png: cream wordmark on transparent, for dark backgrounds (Shop app store header).
- elsewhere-logo-dark.png: black wordmark on transparent, for light backgrounds.
- elsewhere-square-logo-1024.png: square badge, safe for a circle crop.
- elsewhere-shop-cover-1920x1080.jpg: cover, three frames from the homepage campaign reel.

Colors: primary #0C0B0E (night black), contrasting #F1E5D1 (cream); secondary #E7B681 (gold),
contrasting #0C0B0E; accent #A23127 (ember red).
Slogan (28 chars): Apparel for other realities.
Short description (133 chars): Original graphic tees and hoodies for people who are here, but mentally elsewhere. Horror, fantasy, myth and humor, printed to order.
Fonts in the art: Anton (wordmark), IBM Plex Mono (tagline). render_logos.js regenerates the logos.
